Privacy Policy for SheetSniper

This Privacy Policy explains how SheetSniper ("the Extension", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Chrome Extension.

1. Core Architecture: Zero Data Tracking

SheetSniper is built on a 100% client-side architecture. We do not operate external application servers that harvest, store, or analyze your browsing data, product research, or personal data.

  • No browsing history collection: We only execute scripts on Amazon product detail pages (such as amazon.com and amazon.co.jp) to extract product dimensions, weight, price, and category for real-time FBA fee calculations.
  • No external server logging: Your Amazon sourcing data is calculated directly in your browser and sent directly to your own Google Drive account. It never touches our servers.

2. Google OAuth & Google Drive / Sheets Permissions

SheetSniper requests the minimal non-sensitive OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.

  • Purpose: This scope only allows SheetSniper to create a spreadsheet named "SheetSniper Sourcing Log" and append rows that you explicitly choose to save via the "Push to Google Sheets" button.
  • Strict Scope Limitation: SheetSniper CANNOT read, modify, or delete any of your other existing Google Drive files, personal sheets, or documents.
  • Revocation: You can revoke SheetSniper's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Security Settings or via the extension popup.

3. Payments & Subscriptions

SheetSniper offers premium subscription features processed securely by Stripe via ExtensionPay.

  • Payment transactions occur on secure hosted checkout pages managed by Stripe.
  • We do not store, process, or have access to your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details.
  • Your subscription status is linked to an anonymous installation token or email address solely for license verification.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with any third parties or advertisers. We only interact with:

  • Google APIs: Directly connecting your browser to Google Drive and Google Sheets APIs for user-directed sourcing logs.
  • Stripe / ExtensionPay: Exclusively for processing optional Pro subscription payments.

5. Limited Use Requirements

SheetSniper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
