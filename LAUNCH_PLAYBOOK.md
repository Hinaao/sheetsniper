## 1. The Killer Hook: 2026 Q4 Peak Fees & "Phantom Margins"

### Why the Timing is Unbeatable (September – October)
- **October 15 Holiday Peak Fee**: Amazon applies higher fulfillment fees (+$0.32 to +$0.40+ per standard unit) across all items shipped between Oct 15 and Jan 14.
- **Current Seller Activity**: Right now, US Online Arbitrage (OA) sellers are actively sourcing inventory that will arrive at FCs and sell during Q4.
- **The "Phantom Margin" Trap**: Almost all free Chrome extensions and static spreadsheets calculate fees using non-peak (Jan 15) baseline tables and ignore the active 3.5% fuel adjustment or Minimal Split placement fees ($0.21–$0.38/unit).
- **The Financial Pain**: On a standard 50-unit OA purchase:
  - Estimated profit by outdated free tools: **+$240**
  - Actual profit after Q4 Peak + 3.5% Fuel + Placement: **+$195**
  - **Error: -$45 margin leakage on a single ASIN batch.**

### The Positioning Sweet Spot
- **Not Competing with Keepa**: Keepa is the indispensable chart tool for sales rank and price history. SheetSniper sits *alongside* Keepa.
- **Not Competing with Helium 10's Enterprise Features**: Helium 10 is for private label keyword research and costs $99/mo.
- **Owning the "Last 30 Seconds"**: After checking Keepa, the seller enters their buy cost, sees true net profit, and pushes 18 data points directly to their Google Sheet in 1 click instead of alt-tabbing and manual copy-pasting.

## 6. The "Anti-Pattern" Checklist (What NOT to Do)

Avoid these common traps that waste time and yield zero paying sellers:

1. ❌ **Do NOT launch on Product Hunt or Show HN**:
   - Tech bros and indie hackers do not sell physical goods on Amazon US. Conversion rate will be 0%.
2. ❌ **Do NOT market on Japanese Twitter / note**:
   - The tool is calibrated for Amazon.com (US) FBA fees in USD and English. Focus 100% on US/UK/CA sellers.
3. ❌ **Do NOT increase the free push quota**:
   - Keep it at 10 lifetime pushes. 10 pushes is enough to verify that Google Sheets sync works seamlessly, but forces a serious sourcer (who reviews 30–100 items/day) to upgrade within their first sourcing session.
4. ❌ **Do NOT modify manifest or resubmit ZIP during CWS review**:
   - Any re-upload resets the review queue and puts you at the back of the line. Wait for initial approval.
5. ❌ **Do NOT buy fake reviews or trade Pro for CWS reviews**:
   - Google's detection algorithm will flag and remove the extension.
6. ❌ **Do NOT add server infrastructure**:
   - Keep the architecture 100% client-to-Google-API. Zero backend costs means $700 MRR equals ~$650+ net profit after Stripe fees.
