## 1. The Core Value Hook (The "Pain Point" Angle)

Do **NOT** post generic startup launch announcements ("We launched our new tool! Check it out!"). Active Amazon OA/FBA sellers will ignore or ban self-promo immediately.

Instead, lead with **Real Math & Industry Frustration**:
1. **Helium 10 Starter Plan Discontinued**: Sellers are forced to pay $99/mo just to run basic FBA calculations.
2. **2026 Hidden Fees**: Most free calculators have not implemented the 2026 formulas (the active 3.5% fuel surcharge & the Minimal Split inbound placement fee). Sellers are unknowingly overestimating their profit margins by $0.50~$1.50 per unit.
3. **Manual Sheet Copy-Pasting**: Sellers still copy ASIN, title, price, and margins one by one into spreadsheets.

### Rules of Engagement:
- Do NOT post a direct link as a fresh post (moderators will remove it).
- Post a **Value/Data Breakdown** post comparing calculated fees vs actual fees, or respond to existing threads where sellers ask about Helium 10 alternatives or 2026 fee increases.

### Template A: Reddit Discussion Post

**Title**: *Did anyone else notice most free FBA calculators are undercounting 2026 fulfillment fees by ~5-10%? (Breakdown inside)*

**Body**:
```text
Hey guys,

I was auditing my sourcing sheet from this past week and realized my actual FBA disbursements were coming in lower than expected.

I dug into Amazon’s official 2026 fee schedules and found two things most free Chrome calculators are currently missing:
1. The 3.5% fuel & inflation surcharge added to all standard fulfillment fees.
2. Inbound Placement Service Fees: Unless you are shipping 5+ identical cartons to multiple FCs (Optimized split), Amazon charges between $0.21 to $0.38+ per standard unit for Minimal Split.

On an item selling for $24.99 with a $10 buy cost:
- Old/basic calculator estimate: Net Profit $5.80 (ROI 58%)
- Actual 2026 official fees (incl 3.5% fuel + $0.27 placement): Net Profit $5.12 (ROI 51%)

If you're buying 50 units, that’s a $34 margin error on a single product run.

I got tired of crunching these adjustments manually, so I built a lightweight Chrome tool for myself called SheetSniper that calculates with the official 2026 rates on the page and pushes the whole row (ASIN, fees, profit, dims) directly to Google Sheets with 1 click.

The calculator itself is 100% free with no account needed. If anyone wants to test the math or check their recent ASINs against it, happy to share the Chrome Web Store link or look at your numbers.
```
